The site's link-preview image: what a chat, a social network or a search result shows when someone shares a page
(`og:image`). The Sunset waves, drawn from the same data as `components/waves`, with the deck's glass card, the
site's name and its one line. Exactly 1200 × 630, the size Open Graph and X show large; the card stays inside the
centre 1080 × 560, which no platform crops.

It isn't placed on a page. It is screenshotted to a PNG at exactly its size, and the PNG is the site's
`storefront.image` (`media/images/og-image.png`). After a change here, take the screenshot again.

`crop` picks the part of the artwork, as an SVG viewBox on the 100 × 50 artboard, 1200:630 wide.
