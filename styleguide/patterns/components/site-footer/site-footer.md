---
title: Site footer
---

The end of every page: what the site is built with and who made it, each linking to GitHub, and the style guide
it comes from, linking to its workbench. Every layout has it
through the frame they share (`partials/base`), after the page's content. The links come from the site's shared
values (`storefront.site.footer` in the site's config); a part that isn't given is left out.

In the deck (`layouts/deck`) it sits over the waves on the same glass as the header and the cards.
