The editorial roles together, as they meet in a piece of writing: kicker, headline, standfirst, byline,
body text, subheads, pull quote and fine print, in a column at a readable line length (`.t-measure`,
65 characters).

Headings, paragraphs, captions and code take their style from the element itself. Text that is not an
element of its own takes a role class: `.t-display`, `.t-kicker`, `.t-standfirst`, `.t-byline`,
`.t-pullquote`, `.t-fineprint`. Sizes come from the `foundations/type-scale` steps.
