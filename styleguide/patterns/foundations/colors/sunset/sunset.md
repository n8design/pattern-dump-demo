title: Sunset
---

Layered waves from aubergine and plum through magenta and crimson to orange, amber and sand.

**Text colours.** Two inks from the palette itself, rather than pure black and white:

- **Cream** (sand 50) on the dark half, aubergine to crimson: 5.1 : 1 on crimson up to 13.7 : 1 on aubergine.
- **Night** (a deep aubergine, `oklch(16% 0.05 325.9)`) on the light half, flame to sand: 4.8 : 1 on flame
  up to 11.7 : 1 on sand.

Both stay within a few tenths of white and black, and read as part of the palette. Flame is the hinge: only
night (4.8) or black (5.1) passes on it, so keep body text off flame, or use it for large type only.

How to read it: `foundations/palette`.
