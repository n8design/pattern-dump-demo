---
title: Deck
---

A page as a slide deck. A cover (eyebrow, headline, standfirst from the page's frontmatter), then the page's
slides (`components/slide`), one per screen, over the Sunset waves (`components/waves`). A page uses it with
`layout: deck` in its frontmatter.

**Scrolling** snaps gently to each screen; the arrow keys, Page Down and the space bar move through the slides
as they scroll any page. As the page scrolls, the waves drift back into place and each slide's content rises in.
Nothing here needs JavaScript.
